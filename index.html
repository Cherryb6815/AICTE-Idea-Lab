ABOUT THE LAB

What is the AICTE IDEA Lab?

The IDEA Lab (Idea, Design & Entrepreneurship Application) is a hands-on innovation facility established under the AICTE framework at Poornima Group. It gives students direct access to fabrication tools, electronics benches and rapid-prototyping equipment — turning classroom concepts into working prototypes.

Vision

To build a self-sustaining culture of innovation where every student can design, build and test real engineering ideas without barriers.

Mission

To equip students with tools, mentorship and industry exposure that convert raw ideas into deployable products and patent-worthy prototypes.

Objectives

Foster interdisciplinary R&D, host structured hackathons, and build a bridge between academic learning and applied, industry-ready engineering.

HOW A PROJECT MOVES THROUGH THE LAB

Innovation Workflow

01

Idea

Students identify a real problem and frame a testable hypothesis with faculty mentors.

02

Design

Concepts are modeled in CAD, reviewed for feasibility, and refined before fabrication.

03

Prototype

The design is built on lab equipment — 3D printed, machined or wired into a working unit.

04

Testing

Rigorous iteration against real constraints, with mentor review at every checkpoint.

05

Innovation

A validated, demonstrable solution — ready for showcase, funding, or further development.
